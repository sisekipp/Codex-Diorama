# ARES / OUTPOST 07 implementation plan

**Goal:** Add the user-approved third diorama: a working Mars base at dusk, with rich terrain, vehicles, suited astronauts, generated tracks, and a replayable rocket launch.
**Spec:** Approved chat design: crater-edge base, habitats/airlocks/tunnels, greenhouse, open hangar, solar/tank/antenna infrastructure, exploration/cargo/maintenance vehicles, astronauts working/walking, actual moving tracks, launch/countdown/arms/flames/smoke/dust/heat, distant abandoned rover and sampling site, wind/dust/time controls and overview/EVA/launch/landscape cameras.
**Architecture:** Independent createMarsScene controller implements the existing scene interface and optional Mars controls; city builders retain their behavior. Pure terrain/timeline helpers are tested separately. Models, terrain/tracks, actors, base, launch and effects live in focused mars modules and share authored physical materials and existing modelAssembly helpers.
**Constraints:** No new dependencies, remote assets, publishing or changes to the two city scenes' people/weather behavior. Astronauts are confined to Mars. Bounded particle/track counts, scene-owned GPU resources and reduced-motion support. Inline execution in the current checkout; implementation is authorized by the user's approval.
